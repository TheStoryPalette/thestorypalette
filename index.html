ABOUT THE EXHIBITION.

Organized by UCSI Mass Communication Students, The Story Palette is a dynamic graduation showcase that brings together diverse creative voices.

Our mission is to convey different colours and stories to society through a wide range of student artworks. From visual storytelling to brand influence, every piece of work on display represents a unique perspective, blending together to form one vibrant collective canvas.
