ABOUT US

The exhibition themed "The Story Palette" is a dynamic graduation showcase organized by the Mass Communication Students from UCSI University that brings together diverse creative voices.

Our mission is to bring different stories created by students from different pathways into one shared palette. From visual storytelling to brand influence, every piece of work on display represents a unique perspective, blending together to form one vibrant collective canvas.
