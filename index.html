ABOUT US

The exhibition themed "The Story Palette" is a dynamic graduation showcase organized by the Mass Communication Students from UCSI University Kuala Lumpur Campus that brings together diverse creative voices.

Our mission is to bring different stories created by students from different pathways into one shared palette. From visual storytelling to brand influence, every piece of work on display represents a unique perspective, blending together to form one vibrant collective canvas.

About The Exhibition

The Story Palette is a Final Year Project Exhibition presented by the Mass Communication students of UCSI University Kuala Lumpur Campus. Unlike many university programmes, UCSI University’s Mass Communication programme offers three distinctive pathways: Film & Television, Journalism, and Marketing Communication, allowing students to pursue the creative and professional direction that best suits their future. The exhibition brings these different choices together on one platform, creating a unique palette of stories, perspectives and voices.

Here, every student is an independent “artist”, and every graduation project is “a stroke of colour” on the canvas. Each project represents a different way of seeing, thinking and communicating, while the three pathways contribute their own distinctive colours to the collective identity of Mass Communication students.

Exhibition Theme Concept

The Story Palette

"Palette" symbolizes diversity and integration. Every graduate represents a unique color. When these different voices come together, they interweave into this vibrant palette belongings to the graduating cohort.

Red Patch

Film & Television

Symbolizes passionate lighting and shadow, dynamics, and visual storytelling.

Blue Patch

Journalism

Symbolizes the pen, truth, text and in-depth reporting.

Yellow Patch

Marketing Communication

Symbolizes by a megaphone, representing sound, brand and influence.
